BEFORE YOU REGISTER

A few things people ask

Do I need my own laptop?

Yes — bring a laptop that can run Python 3 and Jupyter. We'll share a short setup guide after you register, and volunteers will be around early on Day 1 to help with installs.

I've never written a line of code. Is that a problem?

No — Day 1 assumes zero prior experience. If you already know Python, you can skim Day 1 and jump straight into Pandas on Day 2.

Is there a certificate or credit?

Everyone who attends all four sessions and submits the capstone exercise receives a certificate of completion from KDAG.

What do I need to register?

Just your name, roll number, institute email, and department — the form takes under a minute.
